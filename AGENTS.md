## Development

When starting the dev server, use background mode:

```
astro dev --background
```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.

## Requirements

Create a website that promotes a 3d printing business that is based in sydney australia. Offer multicolour printing, printing in PLA, PETG or TPU, and 3d modelling creation. Prices start from $30AUD/kilo + shipping, request a quote with stl files, materials and area for exact price.

The site main page must have a carousel where i can display photos of prints I have made, and a currently hidden section that I can enable to showcase reviews


Have the site split into 4 pages: The main page with an overview of everything, the services pages which outlines and details the services I offer, the products page which showcases the past prints I have made for other customers and the enquire page that gives the details and the form for an enquiry. Feel free to rename any of the pages to your discression.
